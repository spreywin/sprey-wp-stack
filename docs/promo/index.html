WHY SPREY

Crypto payments without giving up ownership or becoming your own infrastructure team.

Sprey separates three jobs that are often mixed together: your commerce system, the payment-processing infrastructure, and your wallet. That keeps responsibilities clearer and reduces the amount of infrastructure a merchant has to operate directly.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple ownership. Clear responsibilities.

1. Your storefrontWooCommerce or another merchant system owns products, cart, checkout and orders.
2. Sprey ProcessingBTCPay creates or tracks the invoice and exposes payment state to the integration.
3. Payment networkThe customer pays through Bitcoin, USDt or an enabled external Lightning path.
4. Your destinationThe payment reaches the merchant-controlled wallet or payment destination.
The payment itself happens independently of Sprey. Sprey provides hosting, blockchain and payment-state observation, and integration infrastructure. It does not receive, hold, exchange, transfer or otherwise control merchant funds.

COMMERCE + PROCESSING + OWNERSHIP

Use the parts together without coupling them into one monolith.

COMMERCE

Your storefront

Your shop remains responsible for products, customers and order workflow. Sprey WP Stack is the current prepared WooCommerce implementation.

PROCESSING

Sprey-hosted BTCPay

The processing layer creates and observes invoices and reports payment state without becoming the merchant's wallet.

OWNERSHIP

Your wallet boundary

Merchant-controlled payment destinations and signing authority stay separated from the hosted processing infrastructure.
