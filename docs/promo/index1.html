WHY SPREY

Crypto payments without turning your business into a server project.

Use a managed BTCPay-based service while preserving the ownership boundary that matters: customers pay merchant-controlled destinations, and Sprey provides the infrastructure around the flow.

01

Keep control

Private signing authority and merchant wallet ownership stay outside Sprey infrastructure.

02

Skip the maintenance

Sprey operates the hosted payment stack, ingress and service layer so you can focus on selling.

03

Grow from what is proven

New merchant flows are promoted only after they are configured and tested on the reference deployment.

PAYMENT FLOW

Simple ownership. Clear responsibilities.

1. CreateYour store or integration asks BTCPay to create a payment.
2. PayThe customer pays the merchant-controlled destination.
3. ObserveBTCPay observes the payment network and invoice state.
4. ConfirmYour connected storefront receives the resulting payment status.
Sprey provides hosting, observation and integration infrastructure. It does not receive, hold, exchange, transfer or otherwise control merchant funds.

Start with the payment rails that are already verified.

Bitcoin, USDt and external Lightning are the foundation. Additional merchant flows are added as they pass real testing.
